# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project welcoming and respectful for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, respectful, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and evidence; do not attack people.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.
- Respect differing views and focus feedback on the work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized attention, threats, doxxing, and unwelcome conduct are not acceptable. Publishing private information without consent is prohibited. Do not use the project to target or intimidate contributors.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove or edit contributions that violate this Code and may take appropriate action, including warnings, temporary restrictions, or removal from project spaces.

To report an incident, use a private contact method published on the [maintainer profile](https://github.com/wpfyorg). GitHub profiles do not provide direct messaging. If no private channel is available, request one without identifying people or posting incident details. Do not report conduct concerns in a public issue. Include relevant links or context and avoid sharing anyone's private information. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
